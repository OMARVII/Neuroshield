NeuroShield

Photosensitivity protection for the whole web

NeuroShield is on

It checks videos, canvases and animations on the sites you visit, including tabs that were already open, and hides content that flashes faster than your sensitivity allows, usually within a fraction of a second. It stays silent until something flashes.

  • Pick a sensitivity below. Medium follows the WCAG 2.3.1 standard.
  • Press Alt+Shift+H to hide everything on a page instantly.
  • Pin NeuroShield to your toolbar (puzzle icon, then the pin) to see what it's doing on each page.

Run a safe test to see it catch a strobe without anything flashing on your screen.

NeuroShield lowers your exposure to flashing content but can't catch every flash, and it isn't a medical device. Chrome doesn't let extensions run on some pages, such as the Chrome Web Store and browser settings.

    About

    NeuroShield measures every frame against the WCAG 2.3.1 general and red flash thresholds, on your device. Nothing is collected and nothing leaves your browser.

    NeuroShield lowers your exposure to flashing content but can't promise to catch every flash, and it isn't a medical device. If you have photosensitive epilepsy, keep following your doctor's advice.
